Check-in

Check-in for your flight with AVA Airlines is quick and easy. You can check-in online through our website or mobile app, or you can check-in at the airport. Online check-in opens 24 hours before your scheduled departure time and closes 1 hour before departure. During online check-in, you can select your seat, add baggage, and receive your boarding pass. If you prefer to check-in at the airport, please arrive at least 2 hours before your flight's departure time to allow for check-in and security procedures. We look forward to welcoming you on board!
